# **Introduction**

Prompt Engineering helps users communicate effectively with Artificial Intelligence by creating well-structured instructions. Different prompt styles influence the quality, accuracy, and usefulness of AI-generated responses. This experiment focuses on five advanced prompting techniques and demonstrates their application through a smart manufacturing automation use case.

# **Scenario and Use Case**

## **Scenario**

A manufacturing company plans to modernize its production line by implementing Artificial Intelligence, IoT sensors, and embedded systems. The objective is to automate factory operations, monitor equipment continuously, and predict machine failures before they occur.

## **Target Audience**

Manufacturing industries such as automobile production, electronics assembly, pharmaceutical companies, and food processing units.

## **Objectives**

* Increase manufacturing efficiency.
* Reduce equipment failures.
* Enable continuous monitoring of machines.
* Lower electricity consumption through smart automation.

---

# **1. Comparative Analysis Prompt**

## **Definition**

Comparative Analysis Prompt is used to compare two or more concepts by identifying their similarities, differences, strengths, and weaknesses.

## **Prompt Example**

**"Compare AI-based predictive maintenance and traditional machine maintenance in terms of cost, efficiency, accuracy, and downtime."**

## **AI Output Summary**

The AI explains that predictive maintenance uses sensor data and AI algorithms to detect faults early, while traditional maintenance depends on fixed schedules or manual inspection. Predictive maintenance reduces downtime and improves productivity.

### **Advantages**

* Makes comparison easy.
* Supports decision making.
* Highlights strengths and weaknesses clearly.

### **Limitations**

* Requires clear comparison criteria.
* Large comparisons may produce lengthy responses.

---

# **2. Experiential Perspective Prompt**

## **Definition**

This prompting technique asks the AI to respond from the viewpoint of a particular person, profession, or stakeholder.

## **Prompt Example**

**"As a factory manager, explain how AI-based automation improves daily production activities."**

## **AI Output Summary**

The AI answers from the manager's perspective, explaining better production planning, reduced machine failures, lower operational costs, and improved worker safety.

### **Advantages**

* Produces realistic responses.
* Provides practical viewpoints.
* Useful for role-based discussions.

### **Limitations**

* Depends on the chosen perspective.
* May include subjective opinions.

---

# **3. Everyday Functioning Prompt**

## **Definition**

Everyday Functioning Prompt explains how a technology is used in normal day-to-day activities.

## **Prompt Example**

**"Explain how Artificial Intelligence is used in everyday online shopping applications."**

## **AI Output Summary**

The AI describes personalized product recommendations, chatbots for customer support, fraud detection, and smart delivery predictions.

### **Advantages**

* Easy to understand.
* Connects technology with daily life.
* Suitable for beginners.

### **Limitations**

* Covers only common applications.
* May not include technical details.

---

# **4. Universal Prompt Structures**

## **Definition**

Universal Prompt Structures follow a standard template that can be reused for different topics.

## **Prompt Example**

**"Explain how predictive maintenance works in smart factories with a real-life example."**

## **AI Output Summary**

The AI explains the complete process step by step, including data collection using sensors, AI analysis, fault prediction, and preventive maintenance with a practical example.

### **Advantages**

* Produces well-organized answers.
* Easy to modify for different topics.
* Improves consistency.

### **Limitations**

* Generic structure may reduce creativity.
* Needs proper customization for complex tasks.

---

# **5. Prompt Size Limitations**

## **Definition**

Large prompts are divided into smaller sections so the AI can process them efficiently and provide better-quality responses.

## **Prompt Example**

Instead of:

**"Generate a complete 100-page report on smart manufacturing."**

Use:

**"Generate the introduction and objectives of a smart manufacturing report first, then continue with the remaining sections."**

## **AI Output Summary**

The AI generates the report section by section, making the response easier to understand and reducing the possibility of missing information.

### **Advantages**

* Improves response quality.
* Easier to review.
* Reduces processing limitations.

### **Limitations**

* Requires multiple prompts.
* Slightly more time-consuming.
